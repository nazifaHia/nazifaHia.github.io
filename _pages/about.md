I am a **Scientist** at the **Centre for Advanced Research in Sciences (CARS), University of Dhaka**, where I manage the IoT and Robotics Lab and supervise postgraduate research.

My research focuses on developing lightweight and reliable AI methods for resource-constrained systems, particularly in **Domain Adaptation, Edge AI, Computer Vision, Machine Unlearning, and Low-resource NLP**. I also collaborate on software engineering research involving prompt-based code generation and testing.
